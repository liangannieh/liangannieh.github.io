Direct URLs: https://cdn.yoyochinese.com/audio/pychart/<sound>.mp3
Each exact source URL and file hash is recorded in audio/sources.json.

Processing: decoded to mono PCM WAV at the original sample rate; trimmed
outer silence with padding; adjusted volume. Speech speed and pitch are
unchanged. The animation plays the recordings at their original speed.
The optional counting-preview.wav joins the same clips with half-second gaps.

SYNTHESIZED SPEECH
=================

audio/3.wav is a fresh generation of the text "三。" with the
zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural Mandarin voice. Default speed and pitch are preserved.
The original generated MP3 is audio/original-recordings/3.mp3, and its
provenance and hashes are in audio/sources.json. This replaces the earlier
Yoyo Chinese san1 recording.

audio/totals/1.wav through 10.wav are synthesized with Microsoft's
zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural Mandarin voice via edge-tts. They are AI-generated
speech, separate from the human pronunciation clips credited above.
Each phrase uses the parent's selected wording (ge huoche), with liang for two.
Each phrase is generated in full, without joining isolated syllables.
Default rate (+0%) and pitch (+0Hz); trimmed outer silence and leveled volume.
The spoken script, voice, durations, and hashes are in audio/totals/sources.json.
The original generated MP3s are in audio/totals/originals/.
The short endings-preview.wav uses the first three phrases with gaps.
